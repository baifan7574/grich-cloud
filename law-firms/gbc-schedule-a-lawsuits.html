GBC Schedule A lawsuits: what marketplace sellers should organize first

Some marketplace sellers receive notices referencing Greer, Burns & Crain or GBC-related anti-counterfeiting matters. This page is an independent public-record organization guide. JaxFamLaw Records is not affiliated with GBC and does not provide legal advice.

Public fields to verify

Case number, plaintiff, court, filing date, listed counsel, source link and any available defendant list reference.

Seller-provided fields to preserve

Platform notice, store ID, product links, balance screenshots and communication records.

What may remain unresolved

Some attachments may be sealed, delayed, incomplete or require further PACER review by the seller or counsel.
